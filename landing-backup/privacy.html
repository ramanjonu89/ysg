COLLECTION OF PERSONAL DATA

We collect Personal Data from you when:

  1. you register an account on our Platform;
  2. you log in to your account on our Platform or otherwise interact with us via an external service or application (such as Facebook or Google);
  3. you use any of our related services, such as subscribing to our newsletter, marketing collaterals, product or inventory restock notifications;
  4. you browse our products and services or otherwise interact with our Platform;
  5. you use any of the features or functions available on our Platform;
  6. you accept our cookies on your device;
  7. you interact with our customer experience team or other representatives, for example, via our webform or chat function on our Platform, email correspondence, telephone calls, letters, or face-to-face meetings;
  8. you interact with us on our Social Media, such as liking or commenting on our posts, private or direct messaging us on our Social Media;
  9. you record any user-generated content which is uploaded on our Platform;
  10. you participate in our promotions, lucky draws, giveaways, initiatives or any request for additional Personal Data such as customer surveys;
  11. we receive references from business partners and third parties, for example, where they referred you to us to enjoy the benefits of a joint promotion or collaboration or to redeem a voucher you purchased through them;
  12. your authorised representative submits your Personal Data to us for any purpose reasonably authorised by you, for example if such representative is purchasing our product or service to be delivered to you or as a gift;
  13. our third-party analytics and other service providers provide your Personal Data to us, which was collected and processed by them and disclosed to us pursuant to their separate privacy policies; or
  14. when you voluntarily submit your Personal Data to us for any reason.

We usually do not collect your credit card details, whether to process your payment or for customer service purposes. Your credit card details are collected, processed and stored directly by our third-party payment processors pursuant to their terms of use and privacy policies. We may also collect your Personal Data in circumstances where such collection does not require consent under Applicable Laws.

Please ensure that all Personal Data submitted to us is complete, accurate, true and correct. Failure to do so may result in our inability to provide or continue providing you with the products and services you have requested.

ACCESSING OR CORRECTING PERSONAL DATA

You may access or correct your name, birthday, phone numbers, shipping and billing addresses or other Personal Data by logging in to your user account on our Platform.

For Personal Data that cannot be corrected by you by logging in to your user account on our Platform, you may request to access and correct such Personal Data by submitting a written request to us via our [customer service feedback form]] or by emailing our Data Protection Officer at connect@ych.com. We may need to request additional information from you to confirm your identity before providing the access or making the correction.

We will do our best to respond to Personal Data access and correction requests within 21 business days. Where we are unable to meet this timeline, we will update you with the soonest possible time within which we can provide the information or make the correction.

We reserve the right to charge a reasonable administrative fee for retrieving your Personal Data records and/or handling and processing your Personal Data access request. If so, we will inform you of the fee before processing your request.

We will take steps to send your corrected Personal Data to every other person or entity to which such Personal Data was disclosed by YSG within the year after the date of the correction, unless such person or entity does not need the corrected Personal Data for any legal or business purpose.

REQUEST TO WITHDRAW CONSENT

If you wish to withdraw your consent for us to send you sales, marketing or promotional information, please inform us as follows:

  1. to withdraw consent from receiving SMS/Whatsapp promotions or promotions via other social media channels: please unsubscribe by following the steps provided in the promotional SMS/Whatsapp or relevant message/post;
  2. to withdraw consent from promotional emails: please click on the Unsubscribe link in the promotional emails;
  3. to withdraw consent from receiving promotional material with your order package: please email our Data Protection Officer at connect@ych.com.

Once we receive notification of your intention to withdraw your consent for receiving sales, marketing or promotional materials or communications, it may take up to 21 business days for your withdrawal to be reflected in our systems. During this interim period, you may still receive marketing or promotional materials or communications.

If you withdraw your consent to receive marketing or promotional materials through a specific communication mode (e.g. SMS), we may still contact you for other purposes in relation to the products and services via other communication modes you have subscribed to (e.g. email).

You may withdraw your consent for the collection, use and/or disclosure of any of your Personal Data in our possession or under our control by submitting a written request via email to our Data Protection Officer at connect@ych.com. Depending on the nature of the consent withdrawal, we may not be able to continue to provide you with some or all of our products or services. We will process such a request within a reasonable time from the date we receive notice from you of your withdrawal of consent. Once processing is complete, we will no longer collect, use and/or disclose your Personal Data, except to the extent we retain your Personal Data for compliance, regulatory or other legal purposes.

We will make reasonable efforts to ensure Personal Data likely to be used by us or disclosed by us to another organization is accurate and complete. We will also put in place reasonable security arrangements to ensure that your Personal Data is adequately protected and secured. This includes putting in place reasonable measures to prevent any unauthorized access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, leakage, loss, damage or alteration of your Personal Data, such as restricting access to Personal Data to individuals who require access, maintaining technology products to prevent unauthorised computer access and using 128-bit SSL (secure sockets layer) encryption technology when processing your financial details. However, we will not be responsible for any unauthorized use of Personal Data by third parties which is attributable to factors beyond our control.

When Personal Data in our possession is: (a) no longer required for any reason connected to the purpose it was originally collected; or (b) retention by us is no longer necessary for any other legal or business purposes, we will exercise measures to ensure such Personal Data is either destroyed or anonymised.
